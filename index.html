The Problem

Luxury shoppers often visit multiple boutiques and department stores, but every brand maintains separate customer profiles, appointment systems, purchase histories, and communication preferences. Customers repeatedly provide the same information, while Client Advisors spend valuable time recreating profiles instead of building meaningful relationships.

Our Solution

Mesa Ridge Passport provides customers with one secure luxury profile that travels with them across participating retailers. With customer permission, brands can deliver more personalized service while maintaining complete privacy and data security.

Benefits

Benefits for Customers

  • One secure luxury shopping profile
  • Seamless appointment booking
  • Personalized shopping experiences
  • Digital wardrobe and wish list
  • Delivery and alteration tracking
  • Complete control over shared information

Benefits for Retailers

  • Strengthen client relationships
  • Improve appointment management
  • Better customer insights
  • Streamlined clienteling tools
  • Increased customer retention
  • Secure, permission-based customer data

Privacy First

Privacy is the foundation of Mesa Ridge Passport. Customers own their information and decide exactly what they share with each participating retailer. Every retailer operates within its own secure environment, ensuring customer trust and brand confidentiality.
